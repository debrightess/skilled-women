DONATION SETUP

The active website uses only js/paystack-donation.js for card checkout.
Card donations are disabled until the approved foundation's live PUBLIC key is
configured in PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY in that file. Bank transfers remain available.
Never commit a Paystack secret key.

The checkout script loads Paystack InlineJS v2 only when a configured checkout
is requested. The form validates donor details and amounts, converts naira to
kobo, and passes the selected programme and donor information in metadata.
The submit button stays disabled until checkout is cancelled, succeeds or fails.

For sandbox testing, use an isolated development copy with the test public key
and an appropriately adjusted key check; never accept test payments as live gifts.

A client-side callback or thank-you URL is not confirmation of a settled payment.
Verify every donation through the Paystack dashboard. Add server-side verification
or verified webhooks before issuing authoritative receipts automatically.
The thank-you page does not claim payment is verified.

Only js/site.js and js/paystack-donation.js are loaded by the new pages.
The unused legacy js/main.js and root paystack-donation.js files are retained
for compatibility and are not part of the active donation flow.
Do not reintroduce parallel payment handlers.
