A few helpful answers

Before you get in touch.

How do I join a training programme?

Contact us with the skill you are interested in and your location. Our team can advise on available programmes and the next steps.

Can I volunteer or become a mentor?

Yes. Tell us about your experience, interests and availability using the form above or by email.

How can my organisation partner with you?

We welcome conversations about training, mentoring, resources and programme support. Select Partnership above and tell us how you would like to work together.

How can I make a donation?

Visit our donation page for the foundation’s naira and dollar bank accounts and information about card payment.
