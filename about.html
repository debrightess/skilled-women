The Strength of a Skilled Woman Foundation was incorporated by the Corporate Affairs Commission on 26 January 2026 (RC: 8697769).

Based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, we work to empower Nigerian women through vocational training, entrepreneurship support, digital education, health awareness and gender rights advocacy.

Our first-year training target is 500+ women. We are building a community that helps women move from learning a skill to earning an income and shaping their own futures.

Our purpose

What keeps us moving.

Our mission

To empower Nigerian women through skills acquisition, entrepreneurship training and advocacy, creating pathways to financial independence, dignity and community leadership.

Our vision

A Nigeria where every woman, regardless of background, has access to the skills, resources and opportunities to build a life of independence.

Our values

Empowerment. Integrity. Inclusion. Compassion. Excellence. We treat every woman with dignity and value the trust our communities place in us.
